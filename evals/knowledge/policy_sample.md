# Policy Sample Knowledge Base

## Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 7 days after purchase. If the support review approves the request, the refund is returned to the original payment method.

## Support Review Process

Support reviews refund and service requests within 2 business days. The review checks order status, eligibility, and account ownership.

## Billing Contact Path

Billing questions should be sent to billing support. Customers should include the order id, invoice id, and account email when contacting billing support.

## Cancellation Policy

Orders can be cancelled before processing starts. After processing starts, cancellation may no longer be available and the customer should contact support.

## Eligibility Window

Some services have an eligibility window. Requests outside the eligibility window may be rejected even if the customer contacts support.
